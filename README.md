# Guild Ledger: Archero 2 guild tracker

A free tool for Archero 2 guild officers. Record the **Guild Members** screen and the **Monster Invasion → Guild Member Ranking** screen, drop the video in, and it turns them into daily records:
- member power and contribution,
- Monster Invasion attempts (X/2) and damage rankings,
- how each member grows over time.

**How it works:**
- The page runs entirely in your browser. There are no accounts and no server.
- **Reading recordings:** Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, reads the frames through your own Anthropic API key. Add the key once in **Settings**; it usually costs about 2 cents per day of recordings.
- **Your records:** saved only in your browser, on that device.
